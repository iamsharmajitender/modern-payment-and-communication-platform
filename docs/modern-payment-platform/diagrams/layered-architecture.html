Modern payment platform: one rail-agnostic hub, every rail

Read top to bottom to follow a payment. Channels enter the unified payment gateway. The hub calls shared services, sends the payment to one rail adapter, and posts to the ledger after the rail responds.

How to read it

Follow one payment top to bottom

  • A channel calls one Payments API through the edge.
  • The hub validates the payment, calls the services on its left (checks) and right (funds), then picks a rail.
  • The rail adapter speaks that scheme's language to the external network.
  • Status and postings flow right into reconciliation, notifications and reporting.

The one design idea

A rail-agnostic hub

The hub only knows ISO 20022. Every scheme difference lives in an adapter, so a new rail or the BECS wind-down is an adapter change, not a hub rewrite.

Where it gets hard

Three pressure points

  • Fraud and sanctions checks on the accept path, inside the scheme's time limit.
  • A 24/7 core, ledger and liquidity position.
  • Reconciling across six rails.
